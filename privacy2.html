Privacy Policy

At RK Decorators & Caterers, your privacy is our priority. We handle your event details securely.

  • Information We Collect: We only collect your Name, Phone Number, and Event Venue address to coordinate and deliver our decoration services.
  • Payments: Online payments (token amounts/advances) are processed securely via Razorpay. We never store your Credit/Debit Card or UPI PIN details.
  • Data Security: Your personal contact details are kept strictly confidential and are not shared with any third-party marketing agencies.
