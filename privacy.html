Privacy Policy

At Bhopal Properties, your privacy is our priority. We collect minimal data required to provide our services.

  • Information We Collect: We collect your Name, Email, and Phone Number (if you list a property) to allow buyers to contact you.
  • Payments: All payments are processed securely through Razorpay. We do not store your Credit/Debit Card or UPI details on our servers.
  • Data Security: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party marketing companies.
