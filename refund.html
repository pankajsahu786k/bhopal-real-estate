Cancellation & Refund Policy

Please read our cancellation and refund policy carefully before making any payments on Bhopal Properties.

  • Listing Fee: The nominal charge of ₹10 for uploading a property is a one-time, non-refundable digital service fee.
  • Technical Failures: If your payment is deducted from your bank account but the property fails to upload due to a technical server error, the full amount will be automatically refunded to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Cancellation: You can delete your property listing from the dashboard at any time. However, deleting a property will not initiate a refund of the initial listing fee.
