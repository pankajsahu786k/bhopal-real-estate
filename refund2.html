Cancellation & Refund Policy

Please review our cancellation and refund rules before booking your event with RK Decorators.

  • Booking Token / Advance: A minimum advance payment is required to block your date. This token amount is generally non-refundable if the event is canceled by the customer at the last minute, as we block our inventory for your date.
  • Technical Payment Failures: If an online payment is deducted from your bank account but fails to reach us due to a network error, the full amount will be automatically refunded to your original payment method by the banking gateway within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Rescheduling: Subject to availability, you may request to reschedule your event date at least 7 days in advance without losing your token amount.
